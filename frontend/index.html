Security & trust model

Stronger than a sponsor-controlled bounty — by construction

Honest scope. MergeBounty reduces discretion and ambiguity; it does not eliminate all risk. We do not claim the system is “fraud-proof” or unconditionally secure — validator adjudication is bounded by the quality of the stated requirements and the evidence, and every dispute path is explicit and time-bounded rather than absolute.
